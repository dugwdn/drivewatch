# 0006: Not building crash detection or 911 calls

- **Status:** Accepted, 2026-09-29 (recommendation in the research Doug read)

## Decision
DriveWatch doesn't detect crashes or call for help. Recommended instead: the insurer's free teen-driver app, and Life360 or the iPhone's own Crash Detection if the family wants it.

## Why
A homebuilt crash detector that misses a crash is worse than none, and it would carry real liability. It's outside the main job (phone use while moving).
